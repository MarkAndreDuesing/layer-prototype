# Who made what: provenance of the images in the model factsheets

> **Role: attribution audit (2026-11-11).** Many results chain models (one model makes or edits the picture, another splits it, a third relights a layer). A good or bad result belongs to the model that produced *that part*. This page traces the images used in the factsheets; where a factsheet credited a model with another model's work, it is corrected. Verdicts stay the user's.

## 1. The shared input

`prototype/test_scene/fox_cottage.png` was generated by **SDXL-Turbo** (seed 13, 4 steps, `make_test_scene.py`, chosen by the user). Every "split / extract / edit the fox & cottage scene" result starts from that SDXL-Turbo picture. Qwen-Image-2.1 did not make it.

## 2. Direct answers

**Stable-Layers: why for "split", and who made the story composites?** The night and rain composites are **Qwen-Image-2.1 edits** of the SDXL-Turbo scene; the story-composite capability (the edit) is Qwen-2.1's. Stable-Layers' own contribution is only the **split of the edited picture into layers**. On the same night and rain edits at 640 px the base Qwen-Image-Layered and Lightning did not give object layers, and Stable-Layers did (one layer per object, hole-free background); CLD (with hand boxes) and RevealLayer were not compared on the identical 640 px inputs (CLD split the same edits at 512 px, see below). So Stable-Layers' standing is "the splitter that worked on the original and on the edited scenes in the measured comparison", not "a story-composite model". The README matrix and the pipeline plan said "story composite: supported" for it; that is corrected below.

**SDXL-inpaint amodal fill.** The image `sdxl_batch_strip.jpg`, used on the amodal, SAM2 and LayerDiffuse pages, has five panels and **only panels 1 and 2 are SDXL-inpaint**; panels 3-5 (fog, glow, snow) are **LayerDiffuse SDXL** effect layers. The amodal page showed all five under "SDXL-inpaint fills". The strip is now split into two images (section 4). In panels 1-2 the picture itself is not SDXL-inpaint's work either: the background (with the tree, arch and fox taken out) is SAM2 + LaMa, the visible cottage is the SDXL-Turbo scene cut out by SAM2, and SDXL-inpaint only filled the hidden part of the cottage.

**RevealLayer and CLD: how much of their credit was borrowed?**
- RevealLayer's own results (its strips, `fox_reveallayer_*`, `bench_reveallayer_*`, the forest `bg_rgba.png`) are its own: clean filled backgrounds and layers that follow boxes. It also supplied the **input layers** for the move strip and for the per-layer relight strips; there, the move, the shadow, the relight and the night / rain look belong to the other models (section 3).
- CLD's own results are its split (decent layers, slow) and its invented replacement background (a different village). The "good style variation" on the night and rain strip (`cld_night_rain.jpg`) is the **Qwen-2.1 edit's** style: CLD only split those edits. The first-look note credited it to CLD.

**`prototype/kaggle/results_qwen21_fox/sheet.jpg`** (eight tiles, all Qwen-Image-2.1; only two use no image input):

| Tile | Input | What Qwen-2.1 did |
|---|---|---|
| scene_input | SDXL-Turbo picture | nothing (it is the input) |
| extract_fox | the SDXL-Turbo picture | cut the fox out; the fox pixels come from the input, re-rendered |
| extract_cottage | the SDXL-Turbo picture | cut out the cottage with the gate arch; the fox was kept too |
| edit_rgba_fox_to_wolf | Qwen-2.1's own extracted fox | repainted it as a grey wolf in the same pose |
| edit_scene_night, edit_scene_heavy_rain | the SDXL-Turbo picture | re-lit and re-drew the whole scene (moon, lit windows, rain, puddles); the composition comes from the input |
| **t2i_scene_from_caption** | **no image, only the caption** | **entirely Qwen-2.1**: a different scene |
| **t2i_rgba_lantern** | **no image, only a prompt** | **entirely Qwen-2.1**: a transparent lantern |

**IC-Light "together with Qwen-2.1".** In the `bg_relight` strips (`event_bg_relight_night.jpg`, `..._rain.jpg`) the convincing night and rain looks are the **Qwen-2.1 edit of RevealLayer's filled background**; the objects, relit by **IC-Light**, came out pale blue-grey (night) or white-grey (rain) and the cottage no darker than before, so IC-Light did not do the relighting that looks good there ([concepts/editing.md §5](../concepts/editing.md)). Qwen-2.1's relight of the object layers is the working combination (`event_perlayer_retry.jpg`). IC-Light's own measured effect on the fox is a darker, redder fox ([iclight_hue_restore.jpg](../img/iclight_hue_restore.jpg), the last tile of the move strip).

## 4. Corrections made after this audit

- `sdxl_batch_strip.jpg` is replaced on the amodal and SAM2 pages by `sdxl_amodal_panels.jpg` (panels 1-2) and on the LayerDiffuse SDXL page by `layerdiffuse_effects_panels.jpg` (panels 3-5).
- The feature matrix and the pipeline plan no longer list "story composite" as a capability of Stable-Layers, CLD or the other splitters: it is Qwen-Image-2.1's; the splitters are credited with splitting the edited picture.
- The relight page and the plan state that IC-Light failed on night and rain objects in the `bg_relight` strips and that the working relight is Qwen-2.1's per-layer edit.
- `cld.md` credits the night and rain style variation to the Qwen-2.1 edit.

## 5. Rule for new results

Every strip names, per tile, the model that produced it and the input it started from (see [TEMPLATE.md](TEMPLATE.md)); a combined result gets one line per model saying what it contributed.
